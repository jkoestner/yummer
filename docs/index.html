Privacy Policy for Yummer

Last updated: November 8, 2025

Yummer (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App.

Information Collection and Use

Yummer does not collect, share, or transmit any personal or usage data. All nutrition information you enter is stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data, and it is never sent to external servers.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools integrated into the App.

Data Security

Because your data is stored locally, it is protected by your device’s own security settings. We recommend keeping your device secure and using backups if desired.

Children’s Privacy

Yummer does not knowingly collect or share any data from children. The App can safely be used by users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any updates, we will post the new Privacy Policy at this same location.
